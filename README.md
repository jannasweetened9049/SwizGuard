## 🔒 How It Works

SwizGuard combines two powerful technologies. It uses WireGuard for speed and security. It adds an extra layer of encryption that mimics standard TLS traffic. This masking makes your tunnel look like a normal website visit. Most filtering systems cannot detect the difference. This allows you to access the web without interference. Your internet provider sees plain data. They cannot read the contents of your messages or your browsing history.

## 🤝 Troubleshooting

If you encounter errors, restart the application first. This fix solves most common problems. Ensure your firewall allows SwizGuard to reach the internet. You may need to click "Allow" if a Windows security prompt appears during the first launch. Check your clock settings. Inaccurate system time causes connection issues with secure tunnels. Sync your computer clock to your local time zone if you notice problems. Contact your network administrator if you remain on a restricted local network that blocks all VPN attempts. SwizGuard performs well, but some strict environments might require additional setup steps beyond the scope of this guide.
